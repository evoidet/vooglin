Pricing factors

The price depends on the workflow, not the size of your company.

  • Number of steps, tools and integrations
  • Quality and structure of existing data
  • User roles, permissions and approvals
  • Documents, messages and reports to generate
  • Testing, documentation and handover
  • Delivery urgency and support needs

Included

A working system, clearly handed over.

  • Agreed project scope
  • Build and integration work
  • Testing with realistic information
  • Essential documentation
  • Handover and launch support
  • Fixes for anything that does not match the agreed scope

Third-party costs

Software costs stay visible.

Subscriptions, transaction fees, hosting and other third-party services are not hidden inside the project price. Any expected external costs are listed separately before you approve the work.
